# Requirements

## Project Name

ClubHub FPTU

## Problem Statement

Currently, club information is scattered across multiple platforms such as Facebook, Discord, Google Drive, and internal communication channels.

Students face difficulties when:

* Finding clubs
* Tracking club events
* Accessing club resources
* Understanding club history
* Connecting with alumni members

Club leaders also face challenges:

* Knowledge is lost between generations
* Event reports are not centralized
* Communication from IC-PDP is fragmented
* Alumni data is difficult to maintain

## Project Goals

1. Centralize club information.
2. Centralize event information.
3. Preserve club knowledge and experiences.
4. Improve communication between IC-PDP and clubs.
5. Provide AI-powered knowledge search.
6. Build alumni member directory.

## Success Criteria
s
* Students can discover clubs.
* Leaders can manage club information.
* Club knowledge is preserved.
* AI can answer questions from uploaded resources.
* System is deployed and usable.

### In Scope
